About Neo Birth Care Center

Our Story

Founded in 2020, Neo Birth Care Center was established to address the need for accessible maternal health care in underserved communities. We are a team dedicated to reducing maternal and infant mortality.

Our Mission

To provide safe, affordable, and quality maternal and newborn care that empowers mothers.

Our Vision

A community where every mother and newborn receives dignified, professional healthcare.

Core Values

  • Compassion
  • Respect
  • Excellence
  • Community
  • Empowerment
