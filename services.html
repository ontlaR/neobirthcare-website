Our Services

1. Antenatal Care

Comprehensive prenatal checkups, ultrasound referrals, blood pressure monitoring, nutritional counseling, and childbirth preparation classes.

2. Postnatal Care

Support for mothers after birth, wound care, family planning advice, and mental health screening for postpartum depression.

3. Newborn Screening

Weight monitoring, jaundice check, immunization guidance, and early detection of health issues in the first 28 days.

4. Lactation Support

One-on-one breastfeeding counseling, techniques for latching, and management of breastfeeding challenges.
